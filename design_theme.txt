The launcher uses the restored classic interface from wisc-1.2.0.

Keep its top navigation, Mojangles title, large home banner, library cards,
compact profile rows, skin preview, and Options dashboard. The selected theme
comes from colors.THEMES; Earth 2 is the default. Preserve the original rounded
cards and their subtle borders, hover feedback, and selection highlights.

Current mods, modpacks, updater dialogs, notifications, and progress inherit the
selected classic palette through ui_theme.apply_legacy_theme. Keep their useful
behavior integrated into the classic layout. Use Mojangles for the original
launcher title and home banner, and system fonts for page headings, profile
names, controls, and dense details. Retain all bundled font licenses.

Use the classic matte 12px cards with subtle theme borders, 8px buttons and
artwork corners, and visible hover/focus feedback. Page headings are 24px and
share the classic 28px horizontal and 20px top inset. Profiles, mod selection,
mods, packs, details, reviews, and Updates reuse these shapes and text styles.

Use AcrylicSelector for filters and LegacySelector for the classic forms.
Closed fields are 48px high with 5px corners and theme-colored labels/chevrons.
Menus remain acrylic, anchored below the field at the same width, with one
control per filter. Keep keyboard access and selection feedback usable.

Retained controls keep semantic theme roles, including transparent tints and
button styles. Equal hex values must not cause backgrounds to inherit button
text colors after a theme switch. Keep the selected profile, query, filters,
and scroll position across navigation, and start scans after mounting a page.

Keep transitions brief and interruptible. Retain the shared dialog, toast,
progress, scrolling, and drag-state handling. Check 1100x650 and larger windows;
the title bar must remain usable and long pages must scroll.
